Data Deletion Request

ℹ️ Solar Dashboard stores all user data exclusively on your device. No personal data is sent to or stored on any server operated by this app or its developer.

How to delete your data

Method 1 — Instant deletion on your device (recommended)

1
Open Settings on your Android device
2
Go to Apps (or Application Manager) and find Solar Dashboard
3
Tap Storage → tap Clear Data and confirm — all app data is deleted immediately
4
Optionally tap Uninstall to remove Solar Dashboard from your device entirely

Method 2 — Submit a written deletion request

Email us and we will confirm deletion of any developer-held records within 30 days. Include "Solar Dashboard Data Deletion" in your subject line.

✉ Send deletion request email
