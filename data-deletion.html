Solar Dashboard · dumitriualx-lang.github.io

☑ All Solar Dashboard data is stored only on your device. No personal data is stored on external servers by this app.

What data the app stores

  • Your GPS coordinates (used for solar position and weather fetch)
  • System configuration (panel size, battery capacity, orientation)
  • Battery state of charge history
  • Production calibration readings
  • FusionSolar credentials (if connected) — stored locally in Android SharedPreferences
  • Weather and location cache

Option 1 — Delete all data instantly (recommended)

Since all data is on your device, clearing app data removes everything immediately:

1
Open Android Settings on your phone
2
Go to Apps → Solar Dashboard
3
Tap Storage → Clear Data and confirm
4
Optionally tap Uninstall to remove the app entirely

Option 2 — Submit a deletion request by email

If you would like written confirmation that your data has been reviewed and removed, send us an email. We will respond within 30 days.

✉ Send deletion request email

What happens after deletion

  • All locally stored configuration, calibration, and location data is permanently erased
  • FusionSolar credentials are removed from device SharedPreferences
  • No backup copies exist — data is not stored on any server operated by this app
  • Open-Meteo (weather) and Nominatim (geocoding) receive only anonymous API requests with no identifiers
