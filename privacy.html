Solar Dashboard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026
Solar Dashboard does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data with third parties. All data stays on your device.

1. What data is used

Solar Dashboard uses the following data solely to provide its features:

  • GPS location — used on-device to calculate the sun's position and angle relative to your solar panels, and to request local weather data from Open-Meteo. Your coordinates are stored locally on your device (Android SharedPreferences) so the app remembers your location between sessions. They are never stored on any external server.
  • Weather data — your GPS coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, a free open-source weather API, to retrieve local solar irradiance, temperature and cloud cover. Open-Meteo does not require an account and does not associate coordinates with any personal identity.
  • Energy readings — consumption values, battery state of charge, and system configuration you enter are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.
  • Background monitoring data — while the app runs in the background, the monitoring service fetches weather data using your stored GPS coordinates and calculates estimated solar production, battery charge level and energy flows. All results are stored locally on your device in Android SharedPreferences and used to power the app's dashboard and notifications. No background data is transmitted to any server other than Open-Meteo for weather.

2. What data is NOT collected

  • No account or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No advertising
  • No crash reporting sent to external servers
  • No data sold or shared with third parties
  • No personal data transmitted to the developer

3. Background service

Solar Dashboard runs a persistent background service (Android Foreground Service) to provide continuous solar monitoring, even when the app is not on screen. This service:

  • Fetches weather and irradiance data from Open-Meteo approximately every 30 minutes
  • Calculates estimated solar production, battery state and energy flows
  • Stores updated values locally to your device
  • Sends local notifications about solar production windows, surplus energy and battery status
  • Shows a persistent status notification while active (required by Android for foreground services)
  • Restarts automatically after device reboot to maintain uninterrupted monitoring

This service does not transmit any personal data beyond the Open-Meteo weather requests described above.

4. Permissions explained

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Required to determine your location for sun angle calculation and local weather fetching. Without this the app cannot estimate solar production.
INTERNET
Required to fetch weather and irradiance data from Open-Meteo and load the app interface from GitHub Pages.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Optional. Used to send local alerts about solar production windows, appliance scheduling and battery status. No notification data is sent to external servers.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC
Required to run the background monitoring service. Android requires foreground service declaration for apps that perform background data synchronisation tasks.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Allows the app to restart the monitoring service automatically after the device is rebooted, ensuring uninterrupted solar monitoring.
WAKE_LOCK
Allows the background service to briefly wake the processor to perform scheduled monitoring checks and calculations.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Allows the app to schedule precise 30-minute monitoring intervals using Android's AlarmManager. Used as a backup to the foreground service to ensure monitoring continues reliably.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS
Requests that the device does not put the monitoring service to sleep during battery optimisation. This is necessary to ensure time-sensitive solar production alerts (such as surplus energy notifications) fire reliably. Users can revoke this in Android Settings at any time.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Allows the app to check whether an internet connection is available before attempting weather data requests.

5. Third-party services

The only external service contacted by Solar Dashboard is Open-Meteo, an open-source weather API. Only your GPS coordinates are sent, with no personal identifiers attached. Please review Open-Meteo's terms for their data handling practices.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks or tracking services are used.

6. Data retention and deletion

All data (location cache, energy logs, configuration, background monitoring history) is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and browser localStorage. It is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

You can clear all app data at any time without uninstalling via:
Android Settings → Apps → Solar Dashboard → Storage → Clear Data

7. International users

Solar Dashboard is available in 160+ countries. Regardless of your location, the same data practices described in this policy apply to all users worldwide. No personal data is stored on any server in any country — all data remains on your device.

8. Children's privacy

Solar Dashboard does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, no social features and no data collection of any kind beyond what is described in this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact the developer at the email address listed on the Google Play Store page for Solar Dashboard.
